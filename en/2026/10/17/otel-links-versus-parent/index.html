Represent multiple causal relationships with Span links

Represent multiple trace relationships with links and preserve sampling and visibility limits.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-17 · Estimated read 6 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

Multiple sources cannot all occupy a single parent relationship. Span links can represent associations within or across traces, with creation timing relevant to sampling. Arbitrarily choosing the first input as the parent of an entire batch can make a graph look connected while hiding other causal inputs.

Represent the actual operation

The OpenTelemetry 1.61.0 Trace API specification allows links to other SpanContexts in the same or different traces. Creation-time links may inform sampling; links added afterward may not. Verify the language SDK, limits and backend presentation instead of assuming every association will appear.

The project’s specification overview distinguishes parents and links, including links for batches initiated by multiple source Spans. Imagine one operation aggregating independent messages. Selecting only the first source can omit explicit relationships to others. This is an unexecuted modeling example, not a mandatory instrumentation pattern for every messaging system; apply actual semantics and conventions.

Define the input boundary

Batch size, retries and partial success affect relationships. Specify whether a Span covers reading, the whole batch or a substep. Different boundaries can need different structures. Compressing everything into one long Span does not make a backend reconstruct source relationships.

Keep secrets out of links and unnecessary attributes. SpanContext supports tracing relationships; additional data needs actual semantic and minimization rules. Full messages or customer details do not improve causal correctness merely by being attached.

Across queues, establish context extraction, operation boundaries and stable business identity on retry. A new Span does not prove exactly-once execution; tracing does not replace acknowledgement or deduplication. Observation cannot certify business completion.

Creation time affects available evidence

If links matter to sampling, provide known relationships at the SDK’s creation boundary. Later additions may enrich retained records without recovering discarded data. Do not wait indefinitely for every possible source either: describe what is known during actual processing.

Proposed checks cover multiple inputs, within-trace and cross-trace links, partial failure and retry. Inspect exported data, backend representation, sampling, limits and drop information. No SDK or backend tests were executed and no complete-visibility claim is made.

A linked target may still be unavailable because of sampling, retention, export failure or access permissions. A missing navigation target does not prove that business work never happened. Use other task evidence as needed.

Sampling and missing traces discusses that limit. Model the real operation and its sources before selecting parents and links rather than changing facts to improve the graph.
