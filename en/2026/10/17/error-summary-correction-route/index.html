Connect error summaries to the answers needing correction

Connect validation summaries, focus and field messages into an actionable correction path.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-17 · Estimated read 6 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

An error summary should connect problems to specific answers, with coordinated focus and matching inline messages. “Submission failed” announces a problem without helping someone find the correction. Acceptance should examine the correction journey rather than the presence of red text.

Connect each error to its answer

The GOV.UK error-summary guide recommends a summary even for one validation error, moving focus to it, linking each error to the answer and matching its wording to the inline message. GOV.UK Frontend provides the focus behavior. Other implementations must verify their own behavior; copied styling does not establish equivalent interaction.

Imagine a long application whose date lacks a year. A vague date error can make users search or refill correct content. This scenario was not user-tested. Identify the missing part and target the relevant input instead of the whole form.

Composite questions need particular care. A date may have several controls, while a choice group shares one question. Links should reflect actual error ownership. Pointing every item to the first control produces an attractive but unusable summary. Keep field identifiers and links synchronized as the form changes.

Make the focus change understandable

Both reloads and script updates should announce errors and provide a path to them. Color alone may be insufficient for someone away from the top of the page or using assistive technology. Verify keyboard and screen-reader journeys; a screenshot cannot establish usable correction.

Different summary and inline wording can obscure whether they describe one problem. Explain the input and available correction without exposing internal exceptions. Temporary system failures should not be mislabeled as user mistakes.

Preserve recoverable state

Retain correct answers where permitted so one repair does not require restarting. Sensitive fields, attachments and sessions may have different limits. Explain what must be supplied again without silently retaining inappropriate data in pages or logs.

Proposed checks cover one or many errors, composite inputs, dynamically appearing fields and errors disappearing after correction. Verify counts, ordering, links and focus destinations, including controls that no longer exist. Browser and assistive-technology checks were not executed here.

Error summaries help users discover and locate issues; they do not replace server validation. See form validation and trusted outcomes. The component should help people identify the problem, reach the answer and continue the original task, not merely add a red box.
