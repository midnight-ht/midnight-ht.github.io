HTML meter: why is eighty percent storage use not progress?

Distinguish capacity measurement from task progress and explain range, preferred regions, missing data and out-of-range recovery.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-31 · Estimated read 12 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

Key takeaways

  • Meter represents a measurement in a known range.
  • Low and high regions need optimum to express preference.
  • Browser recovery does not validate upstream data.

Using eighty percent of storage does not mean a task is eighty percent complete. HTML meter represents a measurement within a known range; progress represents task completion. Define the quantity, unit and range before deciding whether a larger value means nearing completion or nearing a capacity limit.

Similar bars make different promises

The HTML meter definition covers a scalar measurement in a known range or a fraction, rather than task progress. Current disk usage describes a resource state. Processed files describe completed work. A percentage alone does not choose the element.

The consulted standard was marked updated on 30 October 2026. No browser or assistive-technology checks were run. Default colors are not assumed to match across implementations, and a full bar cannot establish successful upload.

The number eighty needs a name: eighty GiB out of one hundred GiB is usage; eighty files out of a hundred may be work completed. Calling both “progress” leaves users to infer the quantity from context.

A complete capacity sample

This unexecuted HTML uses fictional capacity and thresholds. They are teaching values, not a product quota or operational alert policy. Visible text carries the interpretation without relying on color.

<!doctype html>
<html lang="en">
<meta charset="utf-8">
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width,initial-scale=1">
<title>Capacity gauge example</title>
<h1>Demonstration storage</h1>
<label for="usage">Storage used</label>
<meter id="usage" min="0" max="100" value="80"
       low="60" high="85" optimum="0"
       aria-describedby="usage-detail">80 / 100 GiB</meter>
<p id="usage-detail">80 GiB used out of 100 GiB.
  Review cleanup at 85 GiB; this threshold is a demonstration policy only.</p>
</html>

Lower usage is the preferred direction here, so optimum sits below low. Low and high identify regions, rather than declaring that low is bad and high is good. A measurement with a desirable middle range would use a different optimum relationship. Do not copy capacity semantics into every gauge.

Actual quotas and alert thresholds must come from service policy with explicit ownership and version conditions. The demonstration threshold is not approved production configuration. The element presents the chosen policy; it does not discover a sensible one.

A compression task that has processed eighty of one hundred files instead calls for the progress semantics. The work unit differs, and final completion may still depend on validation. A capacity gauge does not become upload progress because an upload button is nearby.

What happens when the range is omitted?

A default range is not an unknown business range. With neither bound specified, meter uses zero to one. Passing value 80 while omitting max 100 can lead the browser's recovery algorithm to constrain the presented value. A full bar does not establish that eighty was interpreted as eighty percent.

Author data should satisfy the applicable inequalities, including value within the range and low not exceeding high. Browser recovery is not validation of upstream data. If a service accidentally supplies eight hundred and the display quietly clamps it, a plausible-looking gauge can conceal invalid input.

A component contract should include the unit, value and range, plus a policy for missing or invalid data. An explicit unavailable state is preferable here to silently inventing zero. Other recovery choices need an explanation of whether the measurement remains valid.

Can max be omitted until the quota arrives?

If the true upper bound is unknown, do not treat the default zero-to-one range as confirmed capacity. Show an accurate loading statement, or a plain used quantity when that is all you know, then render the gauge with complete data. Inventing a temporary total of one hundred preserves an appearance by manufacturing a fact.

When a plan change alters capacity, update the range, thresholds and explanatory text together. An unchanged used quantity can occupy a smaller fraction after the denominator grows; that does not mean files were deleted. Distinguish quota changes from resource changes.

Unknown versus zero progress concerns a related distinction, but meter does not acquire the same indeterminate-progress model. Unknown work completion and an unknown measurement range call for different decisions.

Must high appear in red?

The attributes describe relationships rather than guaranteeing a particular palette. If a warning matters, supply readable text and appropriate additional cues, then verify the target environment. Someone unable to distinguish the colors should still understand the quantity, total and applicable threshold.

Multiple gauges also need identifiable names. “Usage” alone may not identify which storage space is being measured. Ensure changing data updates descriptions as well as attributes. The sample uses a label and adjacent explanation; actual announcements remain unverified.

Measurement time is part of interpretation. Yesterday’s cached usage combined with a newly changed capacity may not represent one current measurement. Expose the applicable time or state, and update related values together. Unit conversion must cover the quantity, bounds and thresholds rather than changing a GiB label while retaining byte values. Compare raw data, conversions and timestamps before trusting a plausible gauge.

Proposed acceptance covers zero, near-threshold usage, full capacity, out-of-range input, missing bounds and a quota change. Record attributes, effective IDL values, visible explanations and business data. A colored screenshot alone cannot establish those relationships. No checks were executed here. Start a capacity review by asking what is measured, in which unit and within which known range.
