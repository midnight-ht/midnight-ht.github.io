Bound retries after a rate-limit response

Bound rate-limit retries with response hints, cancellation and an overall waiting budget.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-14 · Estimated read 5 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

After HTTP 429, use rate-limit information and a total waiting budget rather than retrying immediately. Retry-After is optional. Without it, the product still needs a defined backoff and stopping policy rather than indefinite attempts.

The status does not define every quota

RFC 6585 describes too many requests over time, explanatory response content, and an optional waiting hint. It does not prescribe user identification or request counting. Limits may concern resources, identities, or broader services; the status alone cannot establish a per-machine allowance.

Imagine several tabs and background tasks sharing an account quota. Independent immediate retries can collectively sustain the limit even when each caller sees few requests. Speed does not grant permission. This example was not load-tested and carries no savings measurement.

Check the response, published quota contract, and request identity. Undocumented scope remains unknown. Nor does 429 alone establish that a side effect never occurred: confirm outcomes under the specific business contract.

Reconcile hints with the caller's budget

Retry-After offers a waiting basis, not a guarantee of success at expiry. Define attempt limits, an overall deadline, and cancellation. If the suggested wait exceeds the interactive task's budget, end that interaction with a clear recovery path rather than promising eventual completion.

Shared quota state across callers is a proposed design choice. Too broad a scope constrains unrelated work; too narrow a scope permits synchronized retries. Any jitter or timing parameters require service-specific evaluation. No optimal settings or throughput gains are claimed.

Explain the stopping result

Retain investigable identity and outcome state when the budget expires. Waiting ending does not itself prove business success or failure. Explain whether a user can query, retry, or resume, without logging secret tokens.

Proposed tests cover present, absent, and excessive hints, cancellation, and concurrent callers; none were run here. See timeouts and business idempotency. Rate limiting controls continued requests; idempotency explains repeated operations.
