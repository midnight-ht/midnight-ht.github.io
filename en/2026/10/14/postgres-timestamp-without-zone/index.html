Choose timestamp types from the meaning of the stored value

Choose timestamp storage from instant or local-time meaning and preserve original zone intent separately.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-14 · Estimated read 5 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

An input offset does not guarantee an offset-aware storage conversion. PostgreSQL interprets the value according to its target type; timestamp without time zone ignores time-zone indications. Decide whether the field represents an instant or local calendar and clock values first.

The type determines interpretation

The PostgreSQL 18 date/time reference distinguishes ignored indications for zone-free timestamps from instant conversion and session-zone display for zone-aware timestamps. The latter does not retain the original named zone. Displayed local text is not a permanent record of the user’s zone.

Imagine an event API sending an offset while its column uses a zone-free type. The client intends an instant, but storage may retain calendar fields. A later environment can interpret them differently. No insertion or conversion was performed here.

Conversely, an appointment defined as local morning may also depend on location, zone rules, and calendar intent. Converting every field to a zone-aware timestamp cannot reconstruct information never stored. Choose types from the business question.

Preserve original zone intent explicitly

If the selected named zone matters, store it separately. Displaying an instant in a zone does not explain why that zone was originally selected. Audit and recurring schedules may need that additional meaning.

Session settings affect unqualified inputs and displayed output. Cover application, migration, and background connections. Different displayed strings need not imply different stored instants; matching strings do not independently establish the same instant.

Do not invent historical meaning

A migration of zone-free history needs evidence about its original region. Assuming one zone for every record can produce a technically successful conversion with changed meaning. Uncertain records need explicit handling rather than fabricated uniformity.

Proposed checks cover specified and missing offsets, different session zones, and uncertain historical sources. They were not executed. See instants and recurring time intent for the separate scheduling decision.
