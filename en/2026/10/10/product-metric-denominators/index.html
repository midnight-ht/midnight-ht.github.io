More clicks, fewer completed tasks? Define the denominator first

Define attempts, completion and observation windows before interpreting a rise in product clicks.

M Midnight Engineering notes on development, quality, security, delivery, and operations. View Author Profile
Updated 2026-10-10 · Estimated read 6 min
Translations
zh-CN en

Article

Product metrics should describe what users accomplish before they describe clicks. More clicks can mean better discovery or repeated failed submissions. Without an attempt unit, completion condition and observation window, the curve cannot justify investment. This document-request example is hypothetical; no analytics or benefits were measured.

Make the benefit falsifiable

Moving an application online might promise faster access to correct documents. Visits measure entry traffic; time from a valid request to receipt measures something closer to that benefit. Review queues and availability also affect it, so do not attribute every change to the form.

GOV.UK’s metrics guide starts with service purpose, derives benefits and hypotheses, and combines user research with multiple data sources. Its government reporting requirements are not enterprise obligations.

A testable hypothesis might say that less repeated entry reduces abandonment without changing approval outcomes. Observe entry, meaningful attempts, valid submission and review. Decide what action a metric informs before collecting extra personal data.

The denominator defines the population

Sessions can split a returning applicant across days; users can merge distinct requests. A business request identifier may work, but define creation, expiry and reopening first. Do not choose a favorable denominator after inspecting the outcome.

Server receipt, validation, acceptance and delivery are different completion stages. Link them using access-controlled identifiers instead of copying personal identity into every event. A missing terminal event is unknown, not automatically failure.

When requests take days, today’s starts and completions are different cohorts. Group by start date, provide equal observation windows and disclose immature cohorts. Choose windows from the service deadline, and version definitions when the process changes.

Explain changes before acting

A falling submission rate calls for checks on lost client events, delayed review states and applicant eligibility, followed by research into actual barriers. Segmentation does not establish causality, and small groups can amplify chance variation.

Consider correct delivery, duplicate effort and duration together so improvement does not merely transfer work to support. If offline outcomes are unavailable, call the measure online submission rather than service completion. Reliability indicators complement product benefit measures.

For the next review, show definitions, data gaps and the planned response. Resolve competing meanings of one successful attempt before debating a target.
