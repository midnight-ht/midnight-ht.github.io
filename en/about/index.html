About Midnight

This engineering notebook follows software from requirements and architecture through development, testing, security, delivery, operations, and continuous improvement.

Articles examine concrete decisions: acceptance criteria, interface contracts, implementation trade-offs, failure-path tests, release compatibility, and incident learning. AI, agents, and automation are part of the toolkit.

Official-source claims, editorial recommendations, hypothetical examples, and recorded practice are distinguished. Older posts retain their historical context; they are not automatically current-version instructions.

The site uses Hexo with the Midnight theme and publishes in Chinese and English.
