Privacy Policy

How this site handles data related to visits, subscriptions, comments, sponsorships, and third-party integrations.

Privacy midnight / privacy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

What we collect

The theme itself does not create user accounts or collect personal identity data by default. If comments, newsletter signup, search, ads, or analytics are enabled, data is handled by the configured third-party or site-owned service.

Newsletter subscriptions

Newsletter forms are frontend entry points. Email addresses, unsubscribe state, and delivery records should be stored by the connected mail service, with an unsubscribe method provided in real use.

Comments and interactions

If Giscus, Waline, Utterances, or another comment system is enabled, comment content, account identifiers, and interaction records follow that service's privacy rules.

Ads and sponsorships

The theme provides clearly marked ad placements. If ad networks or tracking scripts are added, the site should follow local privacy, consent, and disclosure requirements.

Cookies and local storage

The theme may use local storage, session storage, cookies, or window.name to remember color-scheme preference, not for cross-site tracking.

Contact

To access, correct, or delete information stored by site services, contact the site owner with the relevant page, email address, or comment account.

Summary

Midnight is static-first by default and does not require login; subscriptions, comments, ads, and analytics should be connected and disclosed by the site owner.
